Privacy Policy

Clackers for Mac · Last updated 23 October 2026

Short version: Clackers never records what you type. It collects no data, has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network connections of its own.

Your keystrokes

With your permission (Input Monitoring), Clackers is told when a key goes down so it can play a sound. It uses only the key's position, to place the sound left or right and to give larger keys a deeper sound, and then forgets it. Clackers never stores, logs or sends the keys you press or the text you type.

Your microphone

Clackers does not use or listen to your microphone. To pause during calls, it asks macOS whether another app is using the microphone right now. That is a yes or no answer and contains no sound.

What Clackers stores

Your settings, kept on your Mac: the sound pack, volume, the switches in Settings, and the list of apps where Clackers stays quiet. Nothing else.

Purchases

Clackers Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from Apple.

Children

Clackers collects no data from anyone, including children.
